# ADR-0016: The Pi sits at the operator panel on a 5 V PSU in the box, and the Mega runs on the Pi's USB
Status: Proposed (the supplies are the owner's decisions of 2026-10-15; the wiring they imply is issue #164's); Date: 2026-10-19; Scope: `tools/draw_wiring.py` (sheet 1's FB1 loads and Mega supply, `SlotWires()`, `LowerWiring()`, `UpperWiring()`), `docs/wiring` sheets 1, 2A and 2B
Context: The 2026-10-06 panel reference put the Pi 5, the ODrive and three bucks (Mega 5 V on F1-1, Pi 5.1 V 5 A on F1-2, touchscreen supply on F1-3) in the box, and sheets 1 and 2 drew them there. On 2026-10-15 the owner wrote that "the Pi I believe can sit separate on the user interface monitor with the operator panel. The MEGA and screen don't have their own PSUs, and the ODrive lives inside the spincoater's base too", then put the Pi on "the 5 V buck PSU that's already sitting on the rails" and the tower, which "takes 12-24 V DC", on the main PSU. Sheet 3 drew that (the 5 V PSU in the 24 V row, no Pi or ODrive in the box); sheets 1 and 2 kept the old supplies until issue #164.
Decision: The 5 V PSU, on F1-2, feeds the Pi at the operator panel through the lower left grommet. The Mega runs on the Pi's USB, its PWR input unused, and the screen runs on the Pi; F1-1 and F1-3 are spare. The tower runs on F1-4 through four SSRs on the upper deck's SSR rail; the Pi's GPIO 5, 6, 13 and 26 and its ground come in through the upper front grommet beside the Mega's USB and land on the SSR sockets' input screws. The E-stop's NC2 and the home button are wired to the Pi at the panel, so X2 4-5 and X8 are spare and the door cable carries three conductors. The ODrive's 24 V (F2-8 and 0V-P) leaves by the lower left grommet and J11 by the upper front one, as sheet 3 has them.
Rejected: Landing the tower inputs on X8: it has four terminals for five conductors, and a fifth would lengthen the field rail and move it off the packing study's centres (`StudyMatch()`). Running NC2 and the home button through the box: both ends of each are at the panel.
Consequences: Sheet 1 drops the Mega buck and the screen supply; sheets 2A and 2B draw the rest. The Mega is powered only while the Pi's USB port is. The Pi 5 wants 5.1 V at 5 A at the panel, and neither the 5 V PSU's rating nor the drop along its cable has been checked. Besides the door cables, the 5 V feed, the USB and the tower inputs run between the box and the panel.
Revisit if: the Pi moves back into the box, the 5 V PSU cannot hold the Pi 5's load at the panel, or the Mega must stay powered with the Pi off.
