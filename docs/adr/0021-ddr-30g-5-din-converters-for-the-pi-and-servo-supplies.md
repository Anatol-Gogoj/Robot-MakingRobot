# ADR-0021: The Pi's 5 V and the servos' supply are two isolated Mean Well DDR-30G-5 DIN-rail converters at the back of the 24 V row, the servos' set to 5.4 V, with 0V-S tied to star S
Status: Proposed (issue #244); amends ADR-0016's 5 V PSU and the 6 V servo PSU of sheets 1 to 3; Date: 2026-11-23; Scope: `tools/draw_wiring.py` (sheets 1, 2A to 2E, 3 and 4, the BoM), `tools/layout_study/` (blocks PS5 and PS6), `CAD/Control Box`, `docs/wiring`
Context: The box drew two generic modules on printed DIN clips: the 5 V PSU (76 x 76 x 32) that feeds the Pi 5 at the operator panel from F1-2 (ADR-0016), and a 6 V servo PSU (58 x 63 x 23) on F2-9 behind K2, one channel past the 24 V rail. Neither has DIN feet, and ADR-0016 left the 5 V PSU's rating for the Pi 5 (5.1 V at 5 A) unchecked. The owner asked on 2026-11-23 for DIN-mountable parts in their place. The screen's 12 V already comes from a DIN converter of the same family (ADR-0020). No sheet draws a wire from 0V-S (the Mega's GND, driver COMs, endstops) to star S: the old modules' isolation is not documented, and a common-negative 5 V module would have tied the Mega's 0 V to star S through the Pi's USB.
Decision: Both become Mean Well DDR-30G-5 (DDR-30-SPEC, 2026-03-31): 9 to 36 V DC in; 5 V, 6 A, 30 W out; output adjustable 4.5 to 5.5 V; load regulation 1.5 %; 85 % efficiency, 1.5 A input at 24 V full load and 15 A inrush, both typical; overload limit 110 to 150 % of rated power; over-voltage protection 5.75 to 7 V, which shuts the output down until the converter is powered again; 4 kV DC isolation between input and output; 35 x 90 x 54.5 mm on TS35. They stand side by side in the 24 V row against the back ring, each on its own 41.5 mm rail along x that reaches 6.5 mm past its outer end for its USLKG 5 ground block: the servos' (block PS6, converter x 357 to 392, rail from x 350.5, 0.5 mm past cable slot 2) and the Pi's (block PS5, converter x 392.5 to 427.5, rail to the back ring at 434). Feeds are unchanged: F1-2 (3 A) for the Pi's, F2-9 (3 A, behind K2) for the servos'. Each is drawn with its four terminals on its duct end, the face its wires leave from; the outputs sit at the module's far end and their wires run back along its front. References: the servos' -Vo is jumpered to its -Vin, and 0V-S meets star S on its own wire, up cable slot 3 from a spare output of star S (6 of its 9 now used) to 0V-S 4a. The servo PWM from the Mega then has the servo supply's 0 V as its reference; without the tie both would float, since both converters are isolated. The Pi's -Vo stays untied, so the Pi's return current flows in its own pair and the Pi meets 0 V only through the Mega's USB; the screen's -Vo stays untied as ADR-0020 has it, and its premise that the Pi's HDMI and USB reference it to the box's 0 V now holds through this tie. Settings: the Pi's is set so the Pi 5 reads 5.1 V at 5 A at the panel. The servos' is set to 5.4 V, 0.35 V under the over-voltage trip's 5.75 V minimum; the trim's top, 5.5 V, would leave 0.25 V for the load regulation (0.08 V at 5.5 V) and for what a servo returns to the rail when it is driven backwards, which a buck converter cannot absorb. Sizing: at 5.25 V the 30 W limit gives the Pi 5.71 A, 14 % over its 5 A. At 5.4 V it gives the servos 5.56 A; the firmware moves them one at a time. At 30 W the input draws 30 / 0.85 / 22 V = 1.60 A (1.5 A at 24 V by the datasheet, 1.64 A scaled to 22 V), under F1-2's and F2-9's 3 A; at the 150 % limit, 45 W, it draws 2.41 A, so the converter's own limit acts before the fuse, which protects the wire. Cable: the trim's 0.4 V of headroom over 5.1 V at 5 A allows 80 mOhm of loop, 4.7 m each way of 14 AWG stranded copper at 8.43 mOhm/m (2.57 ohm per 1000 ft at 75 C, NEC Chapter 9 Table 8), less the terminal drops.
Rejected: The servo converter in the old servo PSU's spot, one channel past the 24 V rail: its 90 mm depth ends 5.1 mm from the 110 VAC compartment, the strip that carries the 24 V PSU's 12 AWG trunk to F3, its V- to star S and the 0V-P returns to slot 1 (32 mm wide before), and it puts slot 1's drop behind it. A DDR-60G-5 for the servos (10.8 A): it is wider still. A 6 V DIN converter with a 24 V input: none was found among Mean Well's DDR models (their 5 V ones trim to 5.5 V at most) or Phoenix Contact's QUINT DC-DC models with a 24 V input (18 to 29.5 V out). The servos' at 5.5 V: see Settings. One converter for both: the Pi stays on FB1 through an E-stop while the servos must drop with FB2. Tying the Pi converter's -Vo to its -Vin instead of 0V-S to S: the servos' reference would then run through the Pi, its power pair and its USB cable, which are unplugged and replaced in service, not through one wire in the box. Keeping the old modules on purchased DIN clips: the owner asked for DIN parts, and the Pi's module's rating was never shown.
Consequences: The servos run at 5.4 V instead of 6 V; with a linear motor model their stall torque and speed drop about 10 % (5.4 / 6 = 0.90). The gripper's hold and the lid's close are to be checked with the old supply turned down to 5.4 V before buying. If the servo converter's over-voltage protection trips anyway, the servos stop until F2-9 is powered again (an E-stop and a K2 reset), and nothing reports it: dead servos after a lid close point there first. Wiring: one more 0V-S wire up slot 3, the servo converter's jumper, and its outputs reach slot 2 through the duct and the lane behind the old servo bay, whose floor is now free; the lanes keep their bounds round it. Packing study: blocks PS5 and PS6 take the converters' outline (41.5 x 90 x 62 mounted), PS6 runs along x like PS12, and the layout rescores from 38304 to 38903 conductor-mm (+1.6 %); no other block moves. DIN rails: the 5 V rail goes from 76 to 41.5 mm and the servo rail from 58 to 41.5 mm, 11 pieces from the five 18 in rails on hand. Printed clips drop from 11 to 9. BoM: two DDR-30G-5 at TRC Electronics, $35.36 each (read 2026-11-23). Unchanged: the deck plates and their shop drawings, the fuses and the wire gauges. Open: the servo models and their stall currents; the 15 A inrush against the 3 A blade fuses (its duration is not given; ADR-0020 has the same question at 20 A); the datasheet's derating curve is for vertical mounting with no side spacing given, and the converters lie on their backs on the base plate 0.5 mm apart, each losing about 5 W at full load; both converters' far ends, where the output pairs leave, stand 5.1 mm from the 110 VAC compartment edge (y 183.9), so each 14 AWG pair must be dressed back over the module's front without crossing it, 15 mm short of the mains front lane; the 0V-S tie stays loop-free only while ODrive J11's GND is isolated, as `claude.md` calls it, which joins J11's open logic-supply question; the Pi feed's length to the panel against the 4.7 m bound.
Revisit if: the gripper or lid lacks torque at 5.4 V, the servo supply trips on over-voltage, a DIN converter with a 24 V input and a 6 V output turns up, the servos change, the Pi feed runs longer than about 4.5 m, or the deck layout is solved again.
