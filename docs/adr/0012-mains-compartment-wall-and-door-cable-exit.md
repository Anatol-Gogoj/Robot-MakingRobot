# ADR-0012: A bent 5052 wall closes the 110 VAC compartment, and the 110 V door cable leaves through the back panel
Status: Proposed (2026-10-18, for the owner); Date: 2026-10-18; Scope: `tools/draw_wiring.py` (the wall's constants, `WallFlat()`, `WallChecks()`, `DeckSolids()`, `DoorGland`), `docs/wiring` sheets 3 to 5, `deck_base_plate.dxf`, `panel_lower_back_template.dxf`, `wall_mains_flat.dxf`, `bom_frame.csv`; issue #146
Context: ADR-0010 closed the mains compartment with a wall 10 mm outside the mains parts, but sheet 3 drew it only as a line and the BoM listed it as not designed. Two sets of conductors must leave the compartment. The PSU's DC leads go to K2's rail and the fuse rail. X1 sends a 110 VAC cable to the door plate (F0 through the S1 knob to K1's coil, the H1 lamp, the plate PE: sheets 1 and 2), and the packing study's nets leave that cable out.
Decision: Two pieces of 1/16 in 5052, cut from the upper plate sheet's offcut. Each is bent once along its foot into a 14 mm flange on the low-voltage side and screwed to the base plate with M4 x 12 and nyloc nuts. A tab bent at wall B's front end is screwed to wall A's mains face. Each piece ends in a tab that reaches between the bottom and mid rings to 2 mm from the back and right panels. B stops 1 mm short of the right ring, and its tab and a notch in the top of its right end keep 1 mm from the lower module's front right corner brackets (`BracketChecks()`; the review of #148 found the first tab 1.5 mm into two of them). The top stops 3 mm under the upper plate: its 0.9 mm sag under 5 kg, a 0.5 mm short post, and 1 mm, checked by `WallChecks()`. A 40 x 13 mm edge-trimmed pass-through in wall A, centred on the mains duct, takes the DC leads. The 110 V door cable leaves the compartment straight through an M20 cable gland in the aluminum back panel.
Rejected: Crossing the wall with the door cable and leaving with the E-stop loop through the lower left grommet: it runs a mains cable through the low-voltage side and shares a grommet with SELV cables. An acrylic wall: cast acrylic is UL 94 HB, the reason ADR-0009 keeps every deck around the mains metal. The spare 2020 corner brackets as feet: they fix the wall at points, leave the foot open, and put cast brackets on 1.6 mm sheet. One wall bent into an L: three bends in one 528 mm blank on a small brake.
Consequences: The base plate takes six flange holes, drilled through the wall in place, and its PE stud moves to (330, 150) to clear the flange. Two more PE jumpers (still two lower bars), eight more M4 x 12, and one M20 gland are needed, plus a bending brake in the shop. The door cable and the E-stop loop now reach the door plate from different sides of the box. `RunChecks()` compares heights now, so a flange may pass under a part's overhang. A DIN rail or duct screw through the upper plate hangs its nyloc and tail 9.4 mm below it, deeper than the 3 mm gap over the wall, so sheet 5 keeps every such screw 4.8 mm off the wall's top edge (`ThroughBoltKeepOut()`).
Revisit if: the PSU's terminals do not face the 110 V rail (move the pass-through); the door plate sits at the back or at the left wall (reconsider the exit); the shop has no brake; or a test bend shows the K factor is far from 0.42.
