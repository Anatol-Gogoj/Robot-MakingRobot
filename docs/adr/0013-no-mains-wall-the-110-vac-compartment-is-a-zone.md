# ADR-0013: No wall around the 110 VAC parts: the compartment is a zone of the lower deck
Status: Accepted (2026-10-18, the owner's decision); Date: 2026-10-18; Scope: `tools/draw_wiring.py` (`MainsGap`, `MainsZoneX`, `MainsZoneY`, `RunChecks()`, `DoorGland`), `docs/wiring` sheets 3 and 4, `deck_base_plate.dxf`, `bom_frame.csv`; issue #151; rejects ADR-0012's wall
Context: ADR-0010's layout puts the mains parts (the flat 24 V PSU, the 110 V rail, the inlet) in a strip along the lower deck's right wall. ADR-0012 (Proposed) closed that strip with two bent 1/16 in 5052 walls, and its reviews found them hard to fit: the end tab ran into the corner brackets, its length rode on an assumed bend deduction, and a hex key had to reach a bracket screw past the tab. On 2026-10-18 the owner chose no wall.
Decision: No wall. The 110 VAC compartment stays a zone of the lower deck, from a line 10 mm outside the mains parts to the ring. `RunChecks()` keeps every mains part at least 10 mm (`MainsGap`) inside that line, every low-voltage part and duct outside it, and every upper-plate cable slot off it, so nothing low-voltage on the lower deck stands within 10 mm of a mains part. The mains wiring stays in the compartment's own duct, with the separator sheet 2 calls for. The 110 V door cable still leaves through the M20 gland in the aluminum back panel, so it never runs through the low-voltage side or shares a grommet with SELV cables.
Rejected: ADR-0012's bent wall (the owner's call). A plastic barrier: cast acrylic is UL 94 HB, the reason ADR-0009 keeps the decks metal.
Consequences: With no barrier, protection rests on the parts and the wiring. Every 110 VAC terminal must be finger-safe (IP20) or covered: the PSU's terminal cover stays on, and the inlet's tabs get insulated boots. A low-voltage conductor that shares the lower bay should be insulated for at least the mains voltage. The standard clauses on circuits of different voltages in one enclosure (IEC 60204-1; UL 508A for Class 2 circuits) were not checked against their text: check them before the box is built. Switch off at the inlet before lifting the upper module. Removed with the wall: sheet 5, `wall_mains_flat.dxf`, the six flange holes in the base plate, the DC pass-through, `WallChecks()`, `BracketChecks()`, `ThroughBoltKeepOut()` and `WallLugHoles()`. The BoM needs 8 fewer M4 x 12 and nylocs, two fewer PE jumpers, lugs and tooth washers, 94 mm less edge trim, and no bending brake; every pack count and total is unchanged.
Revisit if: a check of the standards finds a barrier is required between the 110 VAC and low-voltage parts in one bay; or a part in the compartment has live terminals that cannot be covered.
