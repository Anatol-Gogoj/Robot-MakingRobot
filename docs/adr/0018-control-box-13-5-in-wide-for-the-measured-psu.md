# ADR-0018: The control box is 13.5 in wide, for the measured 24 V PSU
Status: Accepted (owner, 2026-10-21: the gantry has room for the wider box); Date: 2026-10-21; Scope: `tools/draw_wiring.py` (`BoxW`, `PartSize["PSU24"]`, `PsuX0`, sheet 3's view positions), `tools/layout_study/` (`BoxW`, the PSU block sizes, `layout3b_build.json`), `docs/wiring` sheets 2A, 2B and 3 to 5, the plate and panel DXFs, `bom_frame.csv`, `CAD/Control Box`; issue #202; supersedes the 13 in width of ADR-0008's envelope and the PSU size and position of ADR-0010
Context: The owner measured the 24 V 1200 W PSU on 2026-10-21: 240 x 126 x 65 mm, against the 215 x 115 x 50 assumed since ADR-0010. Lying flat it is 25 mm longer along the right wall, 11 mm wider and 15 mm taller. The height fits: 82.5 mm on rail and clip in the 106 mm lower bay (ADR-0011), 23.5 mm under the upper plate. The footprint does not fit the 13 in box. Along the right wall the PSU, an 18 mm channel and the 110 V rail need 412.5 mm of the 411 between the rings. Across the deck at K2 the stack is 3 (ring clearance) + 47.8 (fuse rail) + 18 (duct channel) + 85.1 (K2) + 8.1 (the lane along the 24 V row's top face) + 10 (`MainsGap`) + 126 (PSU) + 3 = 301 mm, in 290. Moving the 24 V rail 2.9 mm toward the fuse rail passes `RunChecks()`, but sheet 2A then cannot route the top-face wires of K2, Star S and the 0V-P bars, because their lane drops to zero; the 78 mm bars alone would still be 4 mm short.
Decision: Widen the box from 13 to 13.5 in (`BoxW` 342.9, 302.9 inside the frame) and draw the PSU at its measured size, solid on sheet 3. The PSU's front face moves to `RingClear` from the front ring (x 23, was 46.5), so the mains duct's channel at the PSU's terminal face is 16.5 mm, under the study's 18. Every other layout 3b position stays: the PSU's centre line, the 110 V rail on it and the inlet in line with it move 7.4 mm toward the right wall (the box is 12.9 wider, the PSU 11 wider against the same ring), the upper right grommet moves with its wall, and the lane along the 24 V row's top face becomes 10 mm wide. `layout3b_build.json` is rescored at the new centres: 36,368 conductor-mm for its own centres, 36,389 for the drawn ones, at the study's 85.5 mm bay. As drawn, at the 106 mm bay, the layout scores 37,024, against 36,798 before (+0.6 %).
Rejected: Keeping 13 in by moving the 24 V rail against the 110 VAC compartment's edge: it leaves no lane for the 24 V row's top-face wires. Narrowing `MainsGap` beside the PSU's earthed case: the gap is the compartment's only separation (ADR-0013). Re-solving the lower deck at 13 in with the CP-SAT study: a new arrangement, with every slot, lane and sheet drawn again, for a box half an inch narrower.
Consequences: Nothing ordered changes. The plates are still sheared from the 18 x 24 in 5052 sheets, the panels still nest on the three 24 x 18 in acrylic sheets, the 2020 cut list grows 77 mm (5424 mm) and still takes 6 sticks, and the 1/2 in duct grows 11 mm. All five DIN rails on hand are now used, because the 240 mm PSU rail no longer pairs with another piece, so none is spare. The PSU is the tallest lower part (85.5 mm with `PartClear`), still under the grommet's 106 mm bay. Sheet 3's plans start 3 mm higher and its end and side views 0.5 mm lower, to fit the 98 mm plans at 1:3.5. The deck router now refuses a lane with no width instead of dividing by zero. The fan's position and diameter, the mounting holes and the terminal order are still unmeasured: the vent stays a placeholder, and ADR-0010's condition (the PSU mounts on its large face, by holes or a printed cradle) now applies to a 240 x 126 face.
Revisit if: the fan or the terminals are on a face this orientation blocks; the gantry cannot take 342.9 mm across after all; or a lower deck part grows again, in which case rescore the study and regenerate sheet 2A, whose lanes `RunChecks()` does not check.
