# ADR-0015: The box is held to the gantry by four spare cast brackets on its lower posts, and it comes out by lifting 38 mm and sliding forward under a raised middle member
Status: Proposed (issue #158, part A; the owner's answers of 2026-10-18 and 2026-10-19); Date: 2026-10-19; Scope: `tools/draw_wiring.py` (`GantryMembers`, `GantryMidGap`, `MountSlotL`, `PanelScrews()`, `PanelGeom()`, `JointScrews`, `GantryChecks()`, `BuildGantry()`, `BomRows()`), `tools/build_box_model.py`, `docs/wiring` sheets 4 and 5, `panels_acrylic_1.dxf` and `panel_lower_back_template.dxf`, `bom_frame.csv`
Context: Nothing held the box to the gantry (issue #158). The owner's answers: the gantry is 2020 with a 6 mm slot; its members run left to right against the box's front and back faces, in those faces' planes; its lower members stand on the same rubber feet as the box, so their bottom faces are level with the base plate's underside (z 0); measured face to face, the front middle member is 230 over the front lower member and the front top member 130 over the middle one, the back center member is 38 under the back top member, and the two top members are level; the front middle member can move; the joint only has to hold the box in place and may need tools to undo; the box comes out by sliding up and out; the gantry's feet go on T-nuts in the bottom slots. As drawn, the lower front and back panels' bottom screws (z 11.6, heads 5 proud) sat in the lower member's band (z 0 to 20), and the front middle member (z 250 to 270) overlapped the upper panels' top screw heads (z 245.1 to 253.8) by 3.75.
Decision: Use four of the corner bracket kit's spare cast brackets, one at each lower post on the front and back faces. The vertical leg lies on the panel and takes that post's bottom panel screw, moved up from z 11.6 to 30 (M5 x 12 through the leg and the acrylic front panel, M5 x 10 through the leg and the aluminum back panel, each into the post's roll-in T-nut). That screw passes the panel in a 5.5 x 10 vertical slot, because the member's top face stands on the gantry's own feet under another load: the bracket seats on the member within 2.25 either way. The horizontal leg rests on the member's top face with one M5 x 8 into a roll-in T-nut. The lower front and back panels lose their bottom ring screw; the member backs their bottom edge. Raise the front middle member to 315 over the lower one (z 335 to 355, 45 under the top member). The box comes out by lifting 37.95, which takes its feet 5 over a lower member's top face, and sliding forward: its top, z 287.6 with the top panel's screw heads (279.4 + 3.175 + 5), passes 9.5 under the middle member; any lift from 33.0 to 47.4 clears both. `GantryChecks()` refuses anything proud of the front or back face (screw heads, grommet flanges, the door gland, the PE stud, the mains cut-outs with an assumed 5 mm flange, a panel thicker than the lower one, the brackets) that meets a member in that face's plane, a face whose top protrusion comes within 5 of its next member up when lifted, anything over the box's top, a forward pass under 5, a bracket off its post or near the gland, the PE stud or a mains cut-out, and a member-side hole that would miss the member's slot. The gantry's feet go on M5 screws into roll-in T-nuts in the lower members' bottom slots.
Rejected: Lifting straight out the top: the 5 mm socket heads on both faces catch every member above. A 310 gap, the figure first planned: it left out the top panel's 3.175, and the top would clear by 4.5 with the feet clearing by 5. Out the back: it would clear the back center member by 16.5, but over the mains inlet and the door cable. A round hole for the bracket's panel screw: with the bracket also resting on the member, the joint would fix the member's height against the box, and tightening it would pull the box onto the member through the acrylic. Notching the panels to seat the brackets on the posts: the joint is not structural, so the bracket clamps the panel and the panel stays whole. Heavier L brackets: not needed to locate the box.
Consequences: Nothing new to buy: the kit's brackets go 36 of 40 (3 spare with the saw stop), the M5 roll-in nuts 62 of 100 (panels 50, mount 4, gantry feet 8), the M5 x 8 pack 50 of 100; the M5 x 12 and x 10 (2 each) and the 8 foot screws come from the M5 assortment if it carries those sizes, 9 per size. Sheet 5 draws the box in the gantry; `panels_acrylic_1.dxf` (lower front) and the back panel template trade their bottom screw holes for the slots; sheet 4's screw notes list the three mount joints. Off the gantry, the lower front and back panels have no screw along their bottom edge between the posts, so the acrylic front can bow off the bottom ring on the bench. Taking the box out means freeing the door cable at the door, since it is wired to X1 inside. Open: the bracket's hole positions and leg thickness are assumed (10 and 3.5), and at 10 mm the two socket heads overlap 2.85 in the corner, as in every frame bracket, so a measured bracket settles both; the gantry foot screw's length waits on the foot (`GantryFootStack`); the IEC inlet's flange is assumed until the part is chosen; the gantry's lengths and uprights are not drawn; the heights are tape readings; the committed 3D model files wait for a GUI rebuild.
Revisit if: a bracket measures differently (rerun `GantryChecks()` and `ScrewFits()`), a gantry member moves, something is added to the box's front, back or top faces, or the box has to leave another way.
