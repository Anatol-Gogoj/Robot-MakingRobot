# ADR-0011: The lower bay is set by the lower wall's grommet, not by the tallest lower part
Status: Accepted (owner, 2026-10-17); Date: 2026-10-17; Scope: `tools/draw_wiring.py` frame stack (`LowBay`), `docs/wiring` sheets 3 and 4, both plate DXFs, the panel DXFs, `bom_frame.csv`; changes the lower bay and post lengths in ADR-0010's decision and consequences
Context: ADR-0010 set the lower bay to 78.6 mm, the tallest lower part (K2, 75.6 on its rail) plus 3, and kept a 50 mm desk grommet in the lower left wall for the ODrive 24 V feed, the Pi 5 V feed and the E-stop loop. A lower wall's opening runs from the top of the bottom ring to the underside of the mid ring: 78.6 - 2 x 20 = 38.6 mm, so the grommet (50 mm hole, 64 mm retaining ring, 1 mm clear of each rail) cannot fit, and the generator's `PanelChecks()` refuses it. The same 38.6 mm window shows 38.6 of the flat PSU's 50 mm fan face (body at z 19.1 to 69.1 on rail and clip; window 21.6 to 60.2), so the side vent would sit partly behind the mid ring.
Decision: The generator takes the lower bay as the larger of the tallest lower part plus 3 and, when a lower wall has a cable entry, the grommet ring plus 1 mm each side plus both rings: max(78.6, 64 + 2 + 40) = 106 mm. Everything else in layout 3b stays as ADR-0010 placed it.
Rejected: Keeping 78.6 with cable glands in the lower left wall (M20 glands fit the 38.6 mm window): it adds a second entry type against the owner's choice of grommets for every entry, three holes instead of one, and the fan face stays a quarter hidden. Taking the three lower cables up through the upper plate to an upper grommet: they would pass through the upper module, which then cannot lift off without unthreading them, and it adds the deck rise twice to every conductor.
Consequences: Lower posts 86 mm (58.6 in ADR-0010), upper posts 150.2; lower panels 87.6 tall, upper 191.8; 64.7 mm of wire room over the drivers at the 11 in box height (92.1 at 78.6); 35,761 conductor-mm as drawn against 34,912 at 78.6 (2.4 % more, all of it in the deck rise). The lower windows (66 mm) clear 47.5 of the fan face's 50 mm and give the IEC inlet room in either orientation. The box could still shrink to about 10.3 in and keep 47 mm of wire room, not to the 9.2 in ADR-0010 gave.
Revisit if: the owner shrinks the box below about 10.3 in; the lower left entry moves to a smaller fitting or goes away (the bay then falls back to 78.6 by itself); or the grommet calipers differently from its listing.
