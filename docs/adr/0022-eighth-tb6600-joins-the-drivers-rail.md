# ADR-0022: The eighth TB6600, for the U axis of the mask library elevator, stands on the drivers' rail with the other seven; a shield bar beside the motor duct replaces the shield clamp, and cable slot 1 moves behind the drivers' rail
Status: Proposed (issue #264; the shield bar, issue #269); Date: 2026-11-27, revised 2026-11-28; Scope: `tools/draw_wiring.py` (sheets 1, 2A to 2C, 2E, 3 and 4, the upper plate's DXF and shop drawings, the top panel's vents, the BoM), `tools/layout_study/`, `tools/check_wiring_pins.py`, `tools/build_box_model.py`, `docs/wiring`, `CAD/Control Box`
Context: The mask library elevator of the storage study (a twin-column elevator on a new U axis) needs one more stepper driver. The box held seven TB6600s (X, Y1, Y2, Z, A, B, C) on one upper-deck rail at a 34 mm pitch from x 28 to 260. Past them, cable slot 1 (x 267.5 to 280.5, y 156 to 178) with the 24 V feed duct below it, the shield clamp (assumed 22 x 124, at x 283 to 305 from the drivers' front face across the motor duct; not chosen or bought), the 12 V DC-DC (313 to 365.5, its rail to 372.5) and the back-run duct (377.3 to 402.7) filled the row up to the ring's inside face at 437 (434 with RingClear). The drivers block y 154.5 to 250.5 from the front ring to the DC-DC, and the plate behind y 183.9 lies over the 110 VAC compartment, where no cable slot may be (ADR-0013), so the 24 V feeds can pass the drivers' row only beside its back end. The first version of this ADR (2026-11-27) put U on its own rail in the strip between the motor duct and the right wall, turned 90 deg in plan; on 2026-11-28 the owner asked to swap the clamp and U, so that every driver sits on the same rail, and then to terminate the motor shields on a Siemens EC2GB152 ground bar, the kit the PE bars use (five were ordered on 2026-10-18). The upper plate's shop drawings were at revision A, the CHECKED box not yet signed.
Decision: U is the eighth TB6600 on the drivers' rail, at the row's 34 mm pitch, x 266 to 294; the rail now runs from x 23 to 299. The shield clamp gives way to a shield bar, a sixth EC2GB152: each motor cable's drain wire, spliced to a short 14 AWG green lead because the bar's setscrew terminals take 14 to 4 AWG (distributor listings of the EC2GB152), lands on its own terminal (8 of 15), and the bar is bonded to the upper ground bar. A cable with a braid and no drain wire needs a pigtail made from its braid. It lies along x in the strip between the motor duct (y 278.9) and the right ring (319.9 with RingClear), 14 mm short of the RingClear line (`ShieldChan`, for the upper right grommet's motor cable, the tower cable and the PE wires), its back end 2 mm in front of the back run. Its size is not measured: it is drawn at its package's 8.50 x 0.50 in (Menards' shipping dimensions for the 15-position EC2GB15PU), 216 x 13 x 13 mm, dashed, so x 159.3 to 375.3, y 292.9 to 305.9. A terminal bar does not need the cables to cross it, so the motor duct runs unbroken from driver X to the back run, the cables stay in it, and only the drain wires cross to the bar; sheet 2B draws each drain from its driver's motor terminal to its own terminal on the bar. U's cable, nearest the upper right grommet, is the one that leaves by it, past the bar's back end; the other seven take the back run to the upper left grommet. It takes the ground bars' screws (M5, plain nut, tooth washer). Cable slot 1 moves 32.5 mm back, to x 300 to 313 (y 156 to 178 unchanged), into the gap between the drivers' rail end and the 12 V DC-DC's rail, and the 24 V feed duct runs from it to the motor duct at x 300.15 to 312.85, 1.15 mm from each rail end (`FeedX()` refuses less than 1 mm). The screen's 12 V pair runs in the same duct from the DC-DC's output to slot 2. To open the gap the DC-DC moves 3 mm back, to x 316 to 368.5, and its rail reaches 2 mm past its front end instead of 7; the rail runs from 314 to 375.5, its ground block at the back end, 1.8 mm in front of the back run. The driver signal duct stops at x 289, 1 mm short of slot 2. U keeps everything else of the first version: F2-12 (5 A, 14 AWG copper, behind K2), the return to 0V-P 2 (0V-P 1's nine outputs are all used; `ZpTwoAxes`), its COM on the eighth slice of 0V-S (J and the link on slices 9 and 10), Mega pins U_STEP 37, U_DIR 41 and U_ENABLE 49 (PIN_MAP.md lists all three as free), and a PE home run for its case. It takes the same printed clip as the other seven. In the study, DRV becomes 276 mm long, centre (161.0, 216.5), with nets RFB-DRV 8, R24-DRV 8, LOGIC-DRV 32 and DRV-SHIELD 32; DRVU and its nets are gone; SHIELD (216 x 13) is at (267.3, 299.4), rot 0; PS12 (61.5 long) at 344.75.
Rejected: U on its own rail in the right-wall strip, turned 90 deg (this ADR's first version, 2026-11-27): it fit and left both plates unchanged, but it needed a second rail, a new 96 mm clip print, a signal cable along the front ring past driver X and three more top vents, and it stood outside the row's airflow; the owner chose one rail. A 360 deg shield clamp bar lying along x in the strip (tried first on 2026-11-28): the cables would have run along the bar, where such a clamp grips cables that cross it. The same clamp across the motor duct's back end (the second try): it worked, but left 52.4 mm for eight cables and was a part not chosen or bought, while the EC2GB152 is already the box's ground bar; a 360 deg clamp is the better high-frequency termination, but none was bought (owner's choice, 2026-11-28). One of the two upper PE bars as the shield bar: their 26 home runs need both. Slot 1 merged into slot 2 (x 290 to 350, at the same gap's mouth): one slot fewer, but the slot table, `SlotUse` and the shop drawings' D1 to D3 tags would all renumber; moving slot 1 changes one feature. The lower deck's old servo bay: 91.75 mm for a 96 mm part. The driver outside the box at the tower, on F2-12: it takes 24 V and the step signals out of the box. A new CP-SAT solve: with every other block free it would move cable slots and holes the shop drawings fix, so, like ADR-0020 and ADR-0021, the placement is by hand inside the generator's checks and rescored. Making the gantry's front, back and right members the box's walls (issue #266, studied 2026-11-28): all eight drivers would fit in one row in a 458.8 x 322.9 inside, but the box would no longer lift out of the gantry (ADR-0015), and the owner keeps the lift-out; the RMR's future positive-pressure skin is then separate, panels hung in the gantry's front, back and right members around the box.
Consequences: The upper plate goes to revision B (slot D1 moved 32.5 mm back): its DXF and both shop drawings change, and the base plate regenerates byte for byte at revision A. The layout rescores from 43292 conductor-mm (the first version) to 46280 at the study's 85.5 bay, +6.9 % (38903 before U); sheet 3 gives 47038 as drawn. The BoM gains a sixth EC2GB152 (the shield bar) and two more tooth washers; five are on order for the plates and the gantry. The shield bar stands over the 24 V PSU at its front end, which sets its screws' window to the bar's foot + 8.5 to + 21.7 mm (sheet 4); the PE bars' is + 8.5 to + 13.1. The bar's real size, its foot and its terminal pitch must be measured on one of the bars on hand; a bar longer than 216 mm moves its front end toward the front ring, and one wider than 13 mm eats into the 14 mm channel along the wall. The same part is drawn twice at different sizes: the PE bars on their studs are 8 x 30 and 12 x 26 mm placeholders (`GroundBar()`, which only places them), while the shield bar takes the package's 216 x 13. If the bar really is near 216 mm long, the upper PE bar no longer fits on its stud as drawn (it would reach past the right wall), so the measurement can move the PE bars too. The feed duct carries 16 14 AWG feeds and returns and the screen's 18 AWG pair. Acrylic sheet 1 changes: the top panel has three vents over each of the eight drivers in the row and none over the strip. The BoM gains one TB6600 (not priced) and one PE home run, with eight standard driver clips and no 96 mm clip. `tools/check_wiring_pins.py` reports the three U pins as PENDING while the firmware has no U axis; it fails a U pin that another drawn pin or another U pin uses, or that the firmware defines as a different pin or as a macro that does not resolve to a pin number. The drivers' rail keeps its 5 mm ends, which leave no room for its own ground block; that predates this change. No U endstop or limit input is drawn: the storage study would home U on its two through-beams (pins 57 and 58), which are not in the box yet. Nothing is built, bought or cut.
Revisit if: the storage design drops the U axis or moves it to another driver type, the box gets a ninth driver, the measured EC2GB152 does not fit the strip as drawn, the bench shows motor noise that a 360 deg shield termination would cure, the deck layout is solved again, or the upper plate is cut at revision A before this lands.
