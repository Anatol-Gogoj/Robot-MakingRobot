# ADR-0022: The eighth TB6600, for the U axis of the mask library elevator, stands on the drivers' rail with the other seven; the shield clamp moves to the motor duct's back end and cable slot 1 behind the drivers' rail
Status: Proposed (issue #264); Date: 2026-11-27, revised 2026-11-28; Scope: `tools/draw_wiring.py` (sheets 1, 2A to 2C, 2E, 3 and 4, the upper plate's DXF and shop drawings, the top panel's vents, the BoM), `tools/layout_study/`, `tools/check_wiring_pins.py`, `tools/build_box_model.py`, `docs/wiring`, `CAD/Control Box`
Context: The mask library elevator of the storage study (a twin-column elevator on a new U axis) needs one more stepper driver. The box held seven TB6600s (X, Y1, Y2, Z, A, B, C) on one upper-deck rail at a 34 mm pitch from x 28 to 260. Past them, cable slot 1 (x 267.5 to 280.5, y 156 to 178) with the 24 V feed duct below it, the shield clamp (assumed 22 x 124, at x 283 to 305 from the drivers' front face across the motor duct), the 12 V DC-DC (313 to 365.5, its rail to 372.5) and the back-run duct (377.3 to 402.7) filled the row up to the ring's inside face at 437 (434 with RingClear). The drivers block y 154.5 to 250.5 from the front ring to the DC-DC, and the plate behind y 183.9 lies over the 110 VAC compartment, where no cable slot may be (ADR-0013), so the 24 V feeds can pass the drivers' row only beside its back end. The first version of this ADR (2026-11-27) put U on its own rail in the strip between the motor duct and the right wall, turned 90 deg in plan; on 2026-11-28 the owner asked to swap the clamp and U, so that every driver sits on the same rail. The upper plate's shop drawings were at revision A, the CHECKED box not yet signed.
Decision: U is the eighth TB6600 on the drivers' rail, at the row's 34 mm pitch, x 266 to 294; the rail now runs from x 23 to 299. The shield clamp moves to the motor duct's back end: it still lies across the duct, so the motor cables cross it as a shield bar needs, at x 353.3 to 375.3, 2 mm in front of the back run, and reaches from the duct's inner wall (y 253.5) into the strip toward the right wall, to y 305.9. That leaves 14 mm to the RingClear line for what runs along the right wall (`ClampChan`: the upper right grommet's motor cable, the tower cable, the clamp's and the ground bar's PE), so the bar is 52.4 mm long. The motor duct now ends at the clamp; the seven cables to the upper left grommet go on into the back run, and the upper right grommet's cable (x 292) leaves the clamp's wall end and runs forward along the wall. Cable slot 1 moves 32.5 mm back, to x 300 to 313 (y 156 to 178 unchanged), into the gap between the drivers' rail end and the 12 V DC-DC's rail, and the 24 V feed duct runs from it to the motor duct at x 300.15 to 312.85, 1.15 mm from each rail end (`FeedX()` refuses less than 1 mm). The screen's 12 V pair runs in the same duct from the DC-DC's output to slot 2. To open the gap the DC-DC moves 3 mm back, to x 316 to 368.5, and its rail reaches 2 mm past its front end instead of 7; the rail runs from 314 to 375.5, its ground block at the back end, 1.8 mm in front of the back run. The driver signal duct stops at x 289, 1 mm short of slot 2. U keeps everything else of the first version: F2-12 (5 A, 14 AWG copper, behind K2), the return to 0V-P 2 (0V-P 1's nine outputs are all used; `ZpTwoAxes`), its COM on the eighth slice of 0V-S (J and the link on slices 9 and 10), Mega pins U_STEP 37, U_DIR 41 and U_ENABLE 49 (PIN_MAP.md lists all three as free), and a PE home run for its case. It takes the same printed clip as the other seven. In the study, DRV becomes 276 mm long, centre (161.0, 216.5), with nets RFB-DRV 8, R24-DRV 8, LOGIC-DRV 32 and DRV-SHIELD 32; DRVU and its nets are gone; SHIELD (52.4 x 22) is at (364.3, 279.7), rot 1; PS12 (61.5 long) at 344.75.
Rejected: U on its own rail in the right-wall strip, turned 90 deg (this ADR's first version, 2026-11-27): it fit and left both plates unchanged, but it needed a second rail, a new 96 mm clip print, a signal cable along the front ring past driver X and three more top vents, and it stood outside the row's airflow; the owner chose one rail. The clamp lying along x in the strip beside the duct (tried first on 2026-11-28): it kept the assumed 124 mm, but the cables would have run along the bar, where a shield clamp grips cables that cross it, and the screen's 12 V pair was left a 3.15 mm channel between the feed duct and the DC-DC. Slot 1 merged into slot 2 (x 290 to 350, at the same gap's mouth): one slot fewer, but the slot table, `SlotUse` and the shop drawings' D1 to D3 tags would all renumber; moving slot 1 changes one feature. The lower deck's old servo bay: 91.75 mm for a 96 mm part. The driver outside the box at the tower, on F2-12: it takes 24 V and the step signals out of the box. A new CP-SAT solve: with every other block free it would move cable slots and holes the shop drawings fix, so, like ADR-0020 and ADR-0021, the placement is by hand inside the generator's checks and rescored. Making the gantry's front, back and right members the box's walls (issue #266, studied 2026-11-28): all eight drivers would fit in one row in a 458.8 x 322.9 inside, but the box would no longer lift out of the gantry (ADR-0015), and the owner keeps the lift-out; the RMR's future positive-pressure skin is then separate, panels hung in the gantry's front, back and right members around the box.
Consequences: The upper plate goes to revision B (slot D1 moved 32.5 mm back): its DXF and both shop drawings change, and the base plate regenerates byte for byte at revision A. The layout rescores from 43292 conductor-mm (the first version) to 45755 at the study's 85.5 bay, +5.7 % (38903 before U); sheet 3 gives 46513 as drawn. The clamp is not chosen, and the bar now has 52.4 mm for eight motor cables, 6.5 mm a cable in one row: the chosen clamp must take them in that length, on both faces of the bar or in two rows if its saddles are wider. The old 124 mm bar crossed the duct only over the duct's 25.4 mm, the rest of it standing beside the drivers' row. The clamp now stands over K1 on the lower deck, so `OpenScrews()` narrows its screw window from the clamp's base + 8 to + 61.6 mm to + 8 to + 31.1 mm (sheet 4). The feed duct carries 16 14 AWG feeds and returns and the screen's 18 AWG pair. Acrylic sheet 1 changes: the top panel has three vents over each of the eight drivers in the row and none over the strip. The BoM gains one TB6600 (not priced) and one PE home run, with eight standard driver clips and no 96 mm clip. `tools/check_wiring_pins.py` reports the three U pins as PENDING while the firmware has no U axis; it fails a U pin that another drawn pin or another U pin uses, or that the firmware defines as a different pin or as a macro that does not resolve to a pin number. The drivers' rail keeps its 5 mm ends, which leave no room for its own ground block; that predates this change. No U endstop or limit input is drawn: the storage study would home U on its two through-beams (pins 57 and 58), which are not in the box yet. Nothing is built, bought or cut.
Revisit if: the storage design drops the U axis or moves it to another driver type, the box gets a ninth driver, no shield clamp grips eight motor cables in 52.4 mm, the deck layout is solved again, or the upper plate is cut at revision A before this lands.
