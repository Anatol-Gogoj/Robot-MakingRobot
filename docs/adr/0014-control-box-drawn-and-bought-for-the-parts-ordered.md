# ADR-0014: The control box is drawn and bought for the parts actually ordered: a 12-way FB1, aluminum DIN rail, and the ground bars, PE blocks and copper power wire bought
Status: Proposed (issue #158; the owner asked for each BoM change on 2026-10-18, and the 5 V PSU and lower left grommet moves are this PR's); Date: 2026-10-18; Scope: `tools/draw_wiring.py`, `tools/layout_study/` (block size, `layout3b_build.json`), `docs/wiring` sheets 1 to 3, `panels_acrylic_1.dxf` (lower left grommet), `bom_frame.csv`; supersedes the owner-supplied ground bars of ADR-0009
Context: The drawings and `bom_frame.csv` were built from the September plan, not from the two Amazon orders of 2026-09-14. Checked against those orders: FB1 is a 12-way fuse module (153 mm), not the 6-way (93 mm) drawn. The DIN rail on hand is five 18 in slotted aluminum rails; the 16 in steel rails in the BoM were never ordered, and the owner will not cut steel. No ground bar was ordered, though the BoM marked five as owner stock. The two terminal kits hold 8 USLKG 2.5 ground blocks against 13 needed. The 12 AWG hookup wire is copper-clad aluminum, sold as low voltage. Nothing was ordered for F0 or the door cable. Sheet 2 also left the mains L and N to the 1200 W PSU under "everything else: 20 AWG", about 12 A at full load (1200 W / 0.9 / 110 V). The owner confirmed the 12-way FB1 and a 3-light tower with buzzer, and has no other PE blocks.
Decision: Draw FB1 as the 12-way. The fuse rail grows 60 mm, which takes the place of the 5 V PSU at its end and of the lower left grommet beside it. The 5 V PSU moves to the 24 V row against the back ring (x 358 to 434), past cable slot 2 and the base PE stud. The lower left grommet moves from x 292 to 390, past the fuse rail's end, and a new check in `RunChecks()` keeps it there. `layout3b_build.json` takes the new centres and is rescored (35,929 conductor-mm for its own centres). As drawn the layout scores 36,798 conductor-mm, against 35,761 before (+2.9 %; ADR-0011), and 35,949 at the study's 78.6 mm bay, against 34,912; the hand layout scored 57,478. Buy five Siemens EC2GB152 15-terminal ground bars, a 20-pack of Erayco USLKG5N ground blocks (24 to 10 AWG) for every PE terminal, 12 AWG stranded copper THHN (black L, white N, red +24 V, blue 0 V power), a Dinkle DK4-TF(5x20) holder with T1AH250V fuses for F0, 25 ft of 18/4 SJOOW for the door cable, a second pack of feet for the gantry's 8, and TZe-FX231-type flexible ID tape for wire markers on the owner's Brother PT-D220. Sheet 2's gauge note now puts the mains on 12 AWG and says copper only. Use the aluminum DIN rails: screwed down every 150 mm, an 88 N pull between two screws bends a TS35 x 7.5 rail 0.25 mm (steel 0.09); `DinRailBend()` works it out.
Rejected: A spare uxcell 1-in 9-out block as a PE bar: 9 ways for 17 and 26 home runs, insulated from the plate, and the same body as the 0 V blocks next to it. Butting FB1 against FB2 to keep the 5 V PSU at the rail's end: it fits by 1 mm and leaves no wiring room. The 5 V PSU beside the servo PSU: it lands on cable slot 2 and the base PE stud. Steel DIN rail: it buys nothing once the rail is screwed down, and it would need cutting. The CCA wire anywhere in the box: aluminum's resistivity is 1.58 times copper's, so its 12 AWG conducts like about 14 AWG copper.
Consequences: New spend $654.32, up from $406.91 (all priced lines $1,044.05), prices read 2026-10-18. The kits' 8 USLKG 2.5 blocks and 14 of the 15 fuse holders are spares. Not covered: the per-rail PE blocks still have no space reserved on their rails (each needs 6.2 mm), the F0 holder and the USLKG5N are not measured (both stay dashed), the ground bars' feet are not measured (sheet 4 keeps the screw window), and 12 AWG between the PSU and the fuse blocks is protected only by the PSU's own current limit. The box-to-gantry mount is part A of issue #158.
Revisit if: a measured part differs from its assumed size, the fuse rail or the 5 V PSU must move again (rescore the study), or the PE blocks fail a continuity check on the aluminum rails.
