# ADR-0022: The eighth TB6600, for the U axis of the mask library elevator, stands on its own rail along the right wall of the upper deck
Status: Proposed (issue #264); Date: 2026-11-27; Scope: `tools/draw_wiring.py` (sheets 1, 2A to 2C, 2E, 3 and 4, the top panel's vents, the BoM), `tools/layout_study/`, `tools/check_wiring_pins.py`, `tools/build_box_model.py`, `docs/wiring`, `CAD/Control Box`
Context: The mask library elevator of the storage study (a twin-column elevator on a new U axis) needs one more stepper driver. The box holds seven TB6600s (X, Y1, Y2, Z, A, B, C) on one upper-deck rail at a 34 mm pitch from x 28 to 260. Past them, cable slot 1 (x 267.5 to 280.5), the shield clamp (283 to 305), the 12 V DC-DC (313 to 365.5, its rail to 372.5) and the back-run duct (377.3 to 402.7) fill the row up to the ring's inside face at 437 (434 with RingClear), and the upper PE stud stands behind the back run. On the lower deck the old servo bay is free, but between the 24 V feed duct (y 86.15) and the 110 VAC line (y 183.9) it is 97.75 mm deep, 91.75 with PartClear at each end, against a TB6600's 96; ADR-0021 already turned down a 90 mm deep converter there because it left 5.1 mm for the PSU's trunk. The deck plates have shop drawings at revision A (issue #213).
Decision: The U driver is the same TB6600, turned 90 deg in plan so its 96 x 28 base runs along its own rail along x in the 41 mm strip between the motor duct (y 278.9) and the right ring (319.9 with RingClear). It stands on a printed clip of its own, 96 mm long, since the other drivers' clips run across their 28 mm face. The part sits at x 28 to 124, y 283.9 to 311.9. The rail runs from x 23 (RingClear) to 130.5, reaching 6.5 mm past the part's back end for its USLKG 5 ground block (6.2 wide), as `DcDcBlockEnd` does for the DDR-30G-5 rails, and is centred at y 297.86. That centre is 1.5 mm off the strip's middle, so the nuts of its rail screws clear the mid ring's front right corner bracket by 1 mm (HangGaps); the rail is 1.46 mm off the motor duct. Its signal end faces the front ring: its signal cable leaves the signal duct's open front end and runs along the front ring past driver X, in a lane between the ring and driver X, so it never shares the motor duct; the motor duct now starts at x 28, level with driver X's front face, instead of 23. Its power end faces back: the feed from F2-12 (5 A, 14 AWG copper, behind K2 like F2-1 to F2-7), the return to 0V-P 2 (its fifth output of nine; 0V-P 1's nine are all used) and the motor cable pass over the ground block and drop into the motor duct. Its COM lands on a new eighth slice of 0V-S, so J (the ODrive J11 ground) and the link move to slices 9 and 10 and the logic rail is 6.2 mm longer. Its case gets a PE home run like the others. Mega pins: U_STEP 37, U_DIR 41, U_ENABLE 49, the storage study's plan; PIN_MAP.md lists all three as free in the current build. Three vents (4 x 88 mm, 8 mm apart) go over it in the top panel. The study gets block DRVU (107.5 x 35) with nets RFB-DRVU 1, R24-DRVU 1, LOGIC-DRVU 4 and DRVU-SHIELD 4, and SHIELD-UL goes from 24 to 28.
Rejected: The drivers' row: no 34 mm is free before the back ring without moving cable slot 1, the shield clamp, the 12 V DC-DC and the back run, and slot 1 is cut in the plate. The lower deck's old servo bay: 91.75 mm for a 96 mm part. The driver outside the box at the tower, on F2-12: it needs no change in the box, but it takes 24 V and the step signals out of the box; the owner chose to re-solve the deck (2026-11-27). The driver standing across the strip as the others stand: the strip is 41 mm deep. The ground block at the rail's front end: the front end cannot pass RingClear. A new CP-SAT solve: with every other block free it would move cable slots and holes the shop drawings fix, and with them pinned its 9 mm channel on every side of a block (`layout_study.Channel`) asks 53 mm for a 35 mm rail in a 41 mm strip, so it finds no place for DRVU; like ADR-0020 and ADR-0021, the placement is by hand inside the generator's checks and rescored.
Consequences: Both deck plates are unchanged (their DXFs and shop drawings regenerate byte for byte, revision A); acrylic sheet 1 changes, since the top panel gains three vent slots. The layout rescores from 38903 to 43292 conductor-mm (+11.3 %): 2971 for the four DRVU nets, 1250 for the eighth motor cable to the upper left grommet, 168 for the longer logic rail. Two more wires pass cable slot 1, and one more motor cable passes the clamp and the upper left grommet. The strip's free channel along the right wall now starts at x 138.5. The BoM gains one TB6600 (not priced), its new 96 mm clip, one PE home run and 2 more M4 x 12 screws and nyloc nuts. `tools/check_wiring_pins.py` reports the three U pins as PENDING while the firmware has no U axis; it fails a U pin that another drawn pin or another U pin uses, or that the firmware defines as a different pin or as a macro that does not resolve to a pin number. The drivers' main rail keeps its 5 mm ends, which leave no room for its own ground block; that predates this change. No U endstop or limit input is drawn: the storage study would home U on its two through-beams (pins 57 and 58), which are not in the box yet. Nothing is built or bought.
Revisit if: the storage design drops the U axis or moves it to another driver type, the box gets a ninth driver, the deck layout is solved again, or the bench shows the U driver running hot without the drivers' row's airflow.
