# ADR-0010: The control box layout lays the 24 V PSU flat and follows the CP-SAT packing study
Status: Accepted (owner, 2026-10-17), on condition that the PSU can be mounted on a 215 x 115 face (holes there, or a printed cradle); the PSU's assumed size and its x 46.5 front face are superseded by ADR-0018 (2026-10-21), which measured it at 240 x 126 x 65 and moves the condition to its 240 x 126 face; Date: 2026-10-17; Scope: `docs/wiring` sheets 3 and 4, both plate DXFs, the panel DXFs, `tools/draw_wiring.py` layout constants; supersedes the deck stack and post lengths in ADR-0009's consequences
Context: Sheet 3 drawn with every part at its real size, rail included (2026-10-17), showed the PSU standing on its 215 x 50 hole face with its fan on the opposite face, 3 mm under the upper plate; fixing the fan meant widening that gap to a quarter of the fan diameter, out of the 35 mm of wire room over the drivers. The motor shield clamp sat at the back-left while the upper-left grommet was at x 70, so every motor cable ran the deck's length twice. A CP-SAT study (`tools/layout_study/`, results in `results_2026-10-17.json`, figure `docs/wiring/layout_study.png`) placed every rail group at its real footprint with 9 mm wiring channels, a closed mains compartment at the inlet, and fixed or wall-sliding grommets, minimizing conductor-weighted Manhattan wire length from sheet 1's nets.
Decision: Lay the PSU flat (215 x 115 footprint, 67.5 mm tall on rail and clip) with its fan face toward the lower right wall, vented through the lower right panel. Arrange both decks as the study's case 3 ("B, exits free"): lower deck with the fuse rail along the left wall, K2/Star/0V-P behind it, the servo and 5 V PSUs beside them, and the mains strip (flat PSU, 110 V rail, inlet at the back wall near y 250) along the right wall; upper deck in rows along x, field rail by the left wall, Mega and 0V-S next, SSR sockets on their own short rail, drivers on the right with the shield clamp at their back end; grommets moved along their walls to the solved positions. The lower bay becomes 78.6 mm (K2 sets it), the lower posts 58.6 mm.
Rejected: The hand layout (57,478 conductor-mm against 31,790). The best layout with the PSU standing (33,117 conductor-mm, but the fan still blows into the plate). One deck (needs 680 x 330 mm, outside the 18 x 13 in footprint).
Consequences: About 45 % less wire; the fan blows sideways at a vented wall; at the 11 in box height the upper bay gets 92 mm of wire room over the drivers (the box could shrink to about 9.2 in and keep 47 mm). A new flat PSU mount replaces `CAD/PSU DIN mount`. Every layout constant in the generator, both plates' cable slots and PE studs, the grommet and vent positions, the panel heights and the nest plan change, and sheets 3 and 4 are redrawn. The study's centre-to-centre wire measure ignores duct paths and terminal access, so the solved positions are the starting point, checked for a duct path per cable group.
Revisit if: the PSU cannot be mounted flat; the fan's diameter or direction rules out a side vent; the owner shrinks the box height; or a duct path forces a different arrangement, in which case rescore it with `tools/layout_study/layout_study.py` (Score) against 31,790.
