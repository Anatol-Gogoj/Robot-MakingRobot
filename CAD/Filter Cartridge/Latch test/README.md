# Latch test: combo piles

Bench test for the combo-pile mask library. In that design each mask is stored with a fresh
cartridge seated in it, and the combos are stacked in piles. Two latch magnets in each mask's
bottom hold the mask to the steel plate of the combo below. A 10 mm slide in +Y moves them off
that plate and releases it.

This test measures the one number the design depends on: whether a latch strength exists that
does all three of these.
- It holds a mask down when the head lifts the spent cartridge away.
- It lets go after the 10 mm slide.
- It does not lift the combo underneath.

Generated by `tools/build_latch_test.py`. Run it with
`freecadcmd tools/build_latch_test.py`. It takes the mask, cartridge and head-coupling geometry
from `tools/build_filter_cartridge.py` and checks itself before writing anything.

**Hardware**
- **430 stainless plate rings** (0.8 mm, ID 92, OD 109): 4. One for each cartridge and one for the seat.
  - They must be real steel. The printed `PlateStandIn` is not magnetic and is useless here.
  - A CO2 laser cannot cut steel. If no rings exist yet, order them laser-cut, for example from SendCutSend or OSH Cut.
- **3 x 2 mm N52 disc magnets:** 15. Each mask takes 2 latch plus 3 weak. Mount every latch magnet the same way up.
- **6 x 1.75 mm disc magnets:** 12, stacked in pairs, the Gripper-B pick magnet.
- **4 mm chrome-steel balls:** 15. Each mask takes 3, the tool takes 6.
- **Fasteners:** 3 M3 x 16 screws with nuts, 4 rubber feet.
- **Measuring:**
  - a digital luggage scale or force gauge, at least 0.05 N (5 g) resolution;
  - a cord through the tool's hub.

Assembly: press the balls into their pockets, glue the magnets, and bond a plate ring onto each
cartridge and into the seat recess. Screw the seat to the acrylic base with the +Y marks lined up.

## Tests

Run every test on each of the three masks, 10 times each. Record the peak force on the scale.
"Combo" means a mask with a cartridge seated in it. A combo is "aligned" when it is centered on
the seat with its ears on the ear marks.

1. **Strip (the latch must hold).**
   - Setup: one combo, aligned on the seat.
   - Action: put the tool on its plate, balls in the mask ball pairs, and pull straight up by the cord.
   - Pass: the cartridge comes up on the tool and the mask stays on the seat, 10 out of 10.
2. **Release (the slide must free it).**
   - Setup: as test 1.
   - Action: slide the tool, with the combo under it, +Y until its notch reaches the second engraved tick (10 mm). Then pull up.
   - Pass: the whole combo, cartridge plus mask, comes up together, 10 out of 10.
3. **Double pick.**
   - Setup: two combos stacked on the seat, both aligned.
   - Action: slide the top one 10 mm and pull up.
   - Pass: only the top combo comes up, 10 out of 10.
4. **Slide force.**
   - Action: with the tool on an aligned combo, pull it 10 mm in +Y with the scale held horizontal.
   - Record: the peak force. The head has to deliver this through the ball coupling.
5. **Re-arm.**
   - Setup: a bare mask aligned on the seat.
   - Action: lower a cartridge into it with the tool. Hold the cartridge down by hand, which stands in for the ejectors, and lift the tool off.
   - Pass: the cartridge and the mask both stay.
6. **Bare on bare.**
   - Setup: two bare masks stacked directly, aligned.
   - Action: lift the top one by hand.
   - Record: whether the lower one comes along. This decides whether bare masks may ever be stacked.
7. **Storage contact.**
   - Setup: leave a combo with a CNT-coated filter seated for 24 to 48 h.
   - Inspect: the stencil top and the film for marks. The film hangs 0.28 mm over the stencil in storage.

**Choosing a recess.** Pick the recess that passes tests 1 to 3 with the most margin. If none
passes, change the magnet size, not the recess range. The fallback is the spring-fed magazine
with fixed hooks, which uses the same combos.

## Geometry checked by the script

- **Latch pockets:** r 53.0 at 90 and 270 deg.
  - 0.70 mm wall to the frame bore.
  - At least 0.57 mm of skin under the frame top, at the 1.0 mm recess.
  - 24 mm from the weak-magnet pockets.
- **Plate coverage:** aligned, each latch magnet sits 100 % over the plate ring below. After the 10 mm slide it is 0 %.
- **Pull tool on a combo in the held position:**
  - nothing overlaps the mask, the plate, the body or the collar;
  - its ball pairs touch the mask balls;
  - lowering the tool 0.05 mm makes the balls collide (control case).
- **Pick magnets:** all six sit 100 % over the plate ring.
