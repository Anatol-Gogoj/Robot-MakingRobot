# Latch test: combo piles

Bench test for the combo-pile mask library. In that design each mask is stored with a fresh
cartridge seated in it, and the combos are stacked in piles. Two latch magnets in each mask's
bottom hold the mask to the steel plate of the combo below. A 10 mm slide in +Y moves them off
that plate and releases it.

This test measures the one number the design depends on: whether a latch strength exists that
does all three of these.
- It holds a mask down when the head lifts the spent cartridge away.
- It lets go after the 10 mm slide.
- It does not lift the combo underneath.

Three test masks bracket that strength by moving the latch magnet face 0.0, 0.5 and 1.0 mm up
from the mask bottom. The tests below pick one of them, or show that none works.

Generated by `tools/build_latch_test.py`. Run it with
`freecadcmd tools/build_latch_test.py`. It takes the mask, cartridge and head-coupling geometry
from `tools/build_filter_cartridge.py` and checks itself before writing anything.

**Hardware.** The counts are what the test uses; buy about 20 % spares, since small N52 discs chip.
- **Steel plate rings** (ID 92, OD 109): 4. One for each cartridge and one for the seat.
  - Material: G90 galvanized steel, 0.030 in (0.762 mm), cut from
    `CAD/Filter Cartridge/FilterCartridge-Plate-G90-0.030in.dxf`.
  - SendCutSend stocks this sheet. Their 304 stainless is not magnetic, and they do not offer 430.
  - They must be real steel. The printed `PlateStandIn` is not magnetic and is useless here.
  - A CO2 laser cannot cut steel. If no rings exist yet, order them laser-cut, for example from SendCutSend or OSH Cut.
- **3 x 2 mm N52 disc magnets, axially magnetized:** 15 (buy 18 or more). Each mask takes 2 latch plus 3 weak.
  - The 2.0 mm thickness sets the recess, so no other size substitutes; 1/8 x 1/16 in (3.18 x 1.59) does not.
- **6 x 1.75 mm disc magnets:** 12 (buy 14 or more), stacked in pairs, the Gripper-B pick magnet.
  - The tool's pockets are open at the top and their floor sets the magnet face height, so 6 x 2 or
    6 x 1.5 pairs also fit. The pull changes a little with the stack height; record which was used.
  - The 6 x 8 mm magnets on hand fit neither part.
- **4 mm chrome-steel balls (52100):** 15 (buy 18 or more). Each mask takes 3, the tool takes 6.
- **Fasteners:** 3 M3 x 16 screws with nuts, 4 rubber feet.
- **Measuring:**
  - a force gauge or hanging scale with peak hold and 1 g (0.01 N) resolution. Most luggage scales
    read in 10 g steps and hold peaks poorly below about 100 g, which is too coarse here;
  - a kitchen scale for the part weights (1 g resolution);
  - a cord through the tool's hub, and thin thread for the mask bridle of test 1b;
  - calipers with a depth rod, and a fine marker.
- **Safety:** safety glasses (N52 discs chip when they snap together), non-magnetic tweezers, and a
  closed box for loose magnets. Swallowed magnets that attract each other through tissue are a medical
  emergency; keep the parts away from children.

**Printing the masks.** The masks carry the real stencil, `CAD/Exploratory Masks/ThinMask_20261016.STEP`:
- the stencil is 0.5 mm thick at the rim and thinned to 0.1 mm over the Ø75 CNT area;
- it has two L-shaped electrode openings.

So the first layer must be 0.1 mm. That is the FFF setup the ThinMask was made for: a 0.2 mm nozzle on a
smooth plate. The latch tests do not depend on the pattern, so a mask whose thin area prints poorly is
still usable for tests 1 to 7.

## Assembly

1. **Check the prints.** Each mask shows its dots (none, one, two). A 3 x 2 magnet slides into every
   latch and weak pocket by hand, a ball presses into every ball pocket, and a 6 mm magnet drops into
   each tool pocket. Ream a tight pocket with a drill bit turned by hand; do not force a magnet in.
2. **Mark the poles.** Stack all 3 x 2 magnets into one column and mark its top face; every magnet then
   has a marked face of the same pole. Do the same with the 6 mm magnets.
3. **Glue the mask magnets** with CA glue on the pocket wall, never on the exposed face, the marked
   face toward the mask bottom in all three masks:
   - **Latch magnets:** the pockets open at the mask bottom. Push each magnet to the pocket's closed
     end with a non-magnetic pin; its face then sits at the recess under test. After the glue cures,
     measure each face's depth below the mask bottom with the calipers' depth rod and record it.
   - **Weak magnets:** flush with the frame top.
4. **Press the balls** into the mask and tool pockets.
5. **Bond the plates:** one ring onto each of the three cartridges, and one into the seat recess. The
   seat ring must sit flush with the seat top (the recess is 0.76 mm, the same as the plate). Keep
   loose magnets and plates apart while the glue cures.
6. **Mount the seat** on the acrylic base with three M3 x 16 screws, the seat's +Y notch at the
   engraved +Y arrow. Fit the rubber feet.
7. **Check the slide scale.** The seat top carries six grooves just right of its +Y notch, at 56 to
   66 mm from the seat center: one tick per 2 mm of slide, the long ones at 0 and 10 mm. The pointer
   is the corner of the pull tool's +Y notch, which sits at r 56 (the 0 tick) when the tool is on an
   aligned combo. Read it looking straight down; as the tool slides, the tick at its rim is the slide.
   (Seats printed before #227 have no scale: draw the same ticks with a fine marker.)
8. **Seat a cartridge in each mask** to make three combos, one per recess. The pick magnets stay out
   of the tool until P2.

## Terms and recording

- **Combo:** a mask with a cartridge seated in it. **Bare mask:** a mask with no cartridge.
- **Aligned:** centered on the seat (or on the combo below) with its ears on the ear marks.
- **Tool on:** the pull tool set on a combo's plate with its ball pairs on the mask balls, notch at +Y.
- **Slide s:** move the tool, and the combo under it, +Y until the pointer reaches tick s.
- **Pull:** lift the cord straight up, slowly, about 1 s from slack to separation, gauge vertical.
- **Peak:** the gauge's peak-hold reading, in grams; N = g x 0.00981. Zero the gauge with whatever
  hangs from it before contact (the tool, or the bridle), so the peak excludes that weight.
- A trial that goes wrong for an outside reason (cord snagged, combo knocked off its marks) is
  repeated, and the repeat is noted.

## Tests

Run P1 and P2, then tests 1, 1b and 2 to 7 on each of the three masks, then choose a recess, then run test 8
on the chosen mask only. "Mask under test" is the mask in the top or only combo.

### P1. Weights

Weigh one cartridge with its plate (w_cart), each mask bare, and each combo (w_combo).

### P2. Pick magnet orientation

With the tool on a combo, each pick magnet pair sits 9.2 mm sideways and 6.1 mm above a weak
magnet of the mask, with the plate between them. Whether that pair helps or hinders the strip
depends on which way the pairs face, and how the plate changes it is not known. Tape the pairs in with the marked face toward the tool face, run 3 test-1 pulls on the
0.5 mask, then flip all six pairs and repeat. Glue the orientation with the higher strip peaks: it is
the harder case for the latch. Record which it was.

### 1. Strip (the latch must hold)

10 trials.

- Setup: one combo, aligned on the seat.
- Action: tool on, pull.
- Pass: the cartridge comes up on the tool and the mask stays on the seat, 10 out of 10.
- Record: the pass count and the peak of each trial (S, the strip force plus w_cart).

### 1b. Latch pull-off

5 trials.

- Setup: a bare mask, aligned on the seat. Tie three loops of thin thread, one under each ear,
  to one knot above the mask center (a bridle), and hang the gauge from the knot.
- Action: pull.
- Record: the peak of each trial (B, the latch hold plus the mask weight).

### 2. Release (the slide must free it)

10 trials.

- Setup: as test 1.
- Action: tool on, slide 10, pull.
- Pass: the whole combo, cartridge plus mask, comes up together, 10 out of 10.
- Record: the pass count, the peak of each trial (R), and the residual r = R - w_combo. A residual
  well above zero means the latch still grips after the slide.

### 3. Double pick (the combo below must stay)

10 trials.

- Setup: two combos stacked on the seat, both aligned. The mask under test is in the top combo. The
  lower combo uses the 1.0 mask, whose latch has the largest gap and so the weakest hold; when the 1.0 mask is under test, it uses the 0.5.
- Action: tool on the top combo, slide 10, pull.
- Pass: only the top combo comes up, 10 out of 10.
- Record: the pass count, and which mask was in the lower combo.

### 4. Slide scan (where hold turns into release)

3 trials at each slide.

- Setup: as test 1.
- Action: tool on, slide s, pull. Slides s: 0, 2, 4, 6, 8, 10 mm.
- Record: at each slide, how many of 3 pulls lifted the whole combo (0 to 3).
- Pass: 0 of 3 at 0 mm, and 3 of 3 at 8 and at 10 mm.
- Why 8 mm: the release is not symmetric. The latch at 90 deg moves outward and is off the plate
  ring below after 3.0 mm. The latch at 270 deg moves inward and is off only after 8.5 mm, and at
  10 mm it sits 1.5 mm inside the ring bore, still in its fringe field. That magnet governs the
  release, and a lift at 8 mm leaves 2 mm of slide margin.

### 5. Slide force

10 trials.

- Setup: as test 1.
- Action: tool on, run the cord from the hub in +Y, hold the gauge horizontal at the tool's height
  and pull the tool 10 mm. If the tool tips, steady its far edge with one finger, pressing on
  nothing.
- Record: the peak of each trial. The head has to deliver this force through the ball coupling.

### 6. Re-arm

10 trials.

- Setup: a bare mask aligned on the seat, and a loose cartridge on the bench.
- Action: pick the cartridge with the tool and lower it into the mask. With two fingertips on the
  cartridge's plate, never on the mask, which stand in for the ejectors, hold it while you lift the
  tool off.
- Pass: the cartridge stays seated, flush in the mask, and the mask has not moved off its marks,
  10 out of 10.

### 7. Bare on bare

5 trials.

- Setup: two bare masks stacked directly, aligned. The mask under test is on top.
- Action: lift the top one by hand, by its ears.
- Record: how many of 5 lifts brought the lower mask along. The latch magnets of the two masks are
  coaxial and mounted the same way up, so they attract; any lift at all means bare masks are never
  stacked.

### 8. Storage contact

Chosen mask only, once.

- Setup: seat a cartridge with a CNT-coated filter in the mask, aligned on the seat, and leave it
  for 24 to 48 h.
- Inspect: the stencil top and the film, under magnification, for marks or transfer. The model puts
  the film 0.76 mm above the stencil with the printed-flange cartridge; this test checks it.
- Record: the hours stored and what was seen; photograph both faces.

## Choosing a recess

Use the medians of each mask's trials.
1. Keep the masks that passed tests 1, 2, 3, 4 and 6.
2. For each, compute two force margins:
   - hold margin = B / (S - w_cart): the latch hold against the pull of the mask's own weak magnets;
   - release margin = (B + w_cart) / r: what holds the combo below against the residual pull of the
     combo above. If r is at or below the gauge resolution, the release margin is not limiting.
3. Choose the mask whose smaller margin is the largest. On a tie, choose the lower slide force (test 5).

A margin below about 1.5 is thin for parts that will be printed again with other tolerances; say so
in the results. If no mask passes, change the magnet size, not the recess range. The fallback is the
spring-fed magazine with fixed hooks, which uses the same combos. Record the outcome in the results
sheet and in an ADR.

## Lobe hold variant (issue #230)

The weak magnets pull the mask up to the cartridge plate across 1.17 mm: at most about 1.0 N for all
three, against ideal steel. A first bench piece stayed stuck to the elastomer. In the lobe hold variant the head
holds the mask itself, and the release tips push it off.

**How it holds.** Each mask ear carries a 3 x 2 mm magnet on a boss at r 63.5, its top 0.33 mm under a 3 x 2 mm
magnet flush with the face of the head's ball lobe. Facing magnets attract with about 2.4 N per lobe (N52,
surface-charge estimate, an upper bound). The mask has no weak magnets. The cartridge stays held by the pick
magnets on its plate, as before.

**How it lets go.** A finger on each release tip lands on the mask's push pad (41 and 221 deg, out to r 62)
4.0 mm of tip travel before the tip's ball reaches the cartridge plate. Stage 1 pushes the mask 4.0 mm off.
The lobe pull left at that separation is about 0.26 N for all three lobes. The latch then has to hold it:
two latch magnets give at most about 6.7 N flush, 2.2 N at the 0.5 mm recess and 0.88 N at 1.0 mm (ideal
steel, upper bounds). Stage 1 must stop close to where the ball meets the plate: a shorter push leaves more
pull (0.69 N at 2.5 mm), and any further push starts ejecting the cartridge. Stage 2, further travel, ejects
the cartridge. The head side of this is a separate head-side proposal, not part of this repo.

| `LatchTest-LobeHold-PullTool.step` | print, face down, 0.4 mm nozzle | 1 | lobes to r 66.5 with a magnet pocket open at the face |
| `../Lobe hold/FilterCartridge-Mask-LobeHold.step` | print, stencil down, 0.2 mm nozzle | 1 | the production mask in the lobe hold variant (`../Lobe hold/` also has its assembly model) |

The pile seat and the acrylic base are the same parts.

**Magnets.** The lobe set needs 18 more 3 x 2 mm magnets: five in each lobe test mask (two latch, three in
the ears) and three in the tool, plus three for the production mask. Glue the ear magnets marked face up, in every
mask. Glue each tool magnet with the face that attracts a marked face pointing down; check it by holding a mask
under the tool before the glue sets.

**In a pile** the ear magnets of stacked masks sit coaxial, 3.46 mm apart, and attract with about 0.15 N each
(0.46 N for three). That pulls the upper mask down, which helps it stay when the cartridge is stripped. After the
10 mm slide they are no longer coaxial, so the pull on the combo below during a double pick is smaller; test 3
measures it.

**Bench checks for the lobe hold:**
- **L1 Hold:** tool held fixed, combo hanging in it, pull the mask down by the bridle under its ears. Peak = the
  lobe hold. Expect at most about 7 N.
- **L2 Lift off a layer:** press a combo onto a cured layer with the tool and lift. The mask must come up with
  the tool. If it does not, measure the adhesion with the bridle on a bare mask pressed the same way.
- **L1b Residual pull:** as L1, with 4 mm spacers between the ear bosses and the tool lobes. Peak = the pull
  the latch must beat after stage 1.
- **L2 is the reason for this variant;** if the mask stays on the layer here too, more hold is needed before
  anything else.
- **L3 Push-off (stage 1 by hand):** combo latched on the seat, tool on. Press both push pads down with two
  fingers about 4 mm while lifting the tool. The mask must stay on the seat and the cartridge come up.

**How tests 1 to 8 change for lobe masks:**
- Test 1 (strip by a straight pull) cannot pass: the lobe hold is far above the latch. L3 replaces it.
- The hold margin becomes B / L1b: the latch pull-off of test 1b against the residual pull.
- The release margin adds the pile attraction: (B + w_cart) / (r + 0.46 N), with r from test 2.
- Test 7: bare lobe masks stacked directly rest on the lower mask's ear bosses and balls, and their ear
  magnets are 3.46 mm apart.
- Rule out the 1.0 mm recess unless test 1b shows at least about three times the L1b residual.

## Geometry checked by the script

- **Latch pockets:** r 53.0 at 90 and 270 deg, open at the mask bottom.
  - 0.70 mm wall to the frame bore.
  - At least 0.53 mm of skin under the frame top, at the 1.0 mm recess.
  - 24 mm from the weak-magnet pockets.
- **Plate coverage:** aligned, each latch magnet sits 100 % over the plate ring below. After the 10 mm slide it is 0 %.
- **Pull tool on a combo in the held position:**
  - nothing overlaps the mask, the plate, the body or the collar;
  - its ball pairs touch the mask balls;
  - lowering the tool 0.05 mm makes the balls collide (control case).
- **Pick magnets:** all six sit 100 % over the plate ring.
- **Seat:** the ring recess is 0.76 mm deep, the plate thickness.
- **Slide scale:** six ticks at r 56 to 66, 1.06 mm outside the plate recess and 5 mm inside the seat rim,
  clear of the +Y notch; the pull tool's notch corner, the pointer, sits at r 55.96 on the 0 tick.
