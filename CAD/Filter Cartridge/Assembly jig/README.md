# Filter cartridge assembly jig

Generated by `tools/build_cartridge_jig.py` (issue #253); never edit these files by hand.

- `CartridgeAssemblyJig.step`: the jig in print orientation, base on the bed, teeth up.
- `CartridgeAssemblyJig.FCStd`: the jig with a locked cartridge in it, for checking the fit.

The jig holds the collar by its six rim notches, as the collar spanner does, so a person can assemble a cartridge
with both hands on the body. Nothing under the collar touches the CNT area: the jig's bore is open to the bench.
The body needs no guide: once it rests on the collar's lugs, its sleeve is already inside the collar bore, which
centres it (0.25 mm play).

## Set up

Screw the jig to the bench through its four Ø4.5 holes (M4), or clamp it by the base rim. It must not turn when the
body is twisted.

## Assemble a cartridge

1. Put a collar into the jig, flange down. Turn it until its six rim notches drop over the six teeth. It then sits
   flat on the ring and does not turn.
2. Put the filter into the collar, CNT side down, with tweezers on its rim. The collar bore centres it. Do not touch
   the Ø75 CNT area.
3. Lower the body, plate up, onto the collar. It rests on the collar's lugs, centred by the collar bore.
4. Turn the body slowly by hand until it drops by about 2 mm: its bayonet legs are then over the lugs. This takes
   at most 60 deg of turning.
5. Turn the body clockwise, seen from above, the way the arrow on the jig points, by about 22 deg, until it stops.
   Grip the knurl and the plate edge. The ramp pulls the body down and clamps the filter rim.
6. Lift the finished cartridge straight up. The collar comes off the teeth with it.

To take a cartridge apart, set it back in the jig, turn the body anticlockwise about 22 deg and lift it off.

## Checks the generator makes

- The collar as printed (locked gap -0.10) rests on the ring without overlap. Turned 0.5 deg either way, it runs
  into the teeth. The teeth keep their full width up to 1.0 mm above the collar bottom (the spanner's narrow from
  0.7), so the locking torque bears on 0.7 mm of each notch flank.
- The body clears the jig along the whole twist, by 0.4 mm or more. The sleeve face meets the filter rim right above
  the ring; that gap is the flange (0.2 mm after the 0.1 mm squeeze).
- A body resting on the lug tops, while it searches for its legs, touches nothing in the jig. Moved 0.3 mm off
  centre there, it runs into the collar: the collar bore centres it.
- The CNT film clears the jig by 2.5 mm. The filter clears the teeth by 0.45 mm; under it is only the flange.
- The jig is one valid solid that fits a 256 mm bed, and its STEP reads back the same.
